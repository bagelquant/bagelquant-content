The complete BagelQuant workflow is:

1. Build point-in-time [data](../research/data.md).
2. Design and evaluate [factors](../research/factors.md).
3. Combine signals into [predictions](../research/prediction.md).
4. Translate forecasts into a [portfolio](../research/portfolio.md).
5. Run a cost-aware [backtest](../research/backtest.md).

Use [Learn](../learn/index.md) when you need theory and [Research](../research/index.md) when you want a practical sequence.
